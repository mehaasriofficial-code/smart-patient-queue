How it works

Four steps carry a patient from check-in to medicine tracking, all saved locally in the browser.

Step 01

Register at the desk

Enter name, age, phone and department. A queue token is generated instantly.

Step 02

Track your position

Watch the live queue move and see your estimated waiting time update.

Step 03

Doctor adds a prescription

The doctor logs medicines, dosage, timing and duration against your token.

Step 04

Follow the medicine plan

Mark each dose taken or missed and see the full schedule on your dashboard.

Built around three everyday clinic problems

Each feature mirrors a real front-desk task — just without the paperwork.

Smart patient queue

A digital token system replaces the paper slip, with live position and wait-time estimates.

Medicine reminders

Every dose gets a time slot and a status — taken, missed or upcoming — tracked on a visual timeline.

Doctor prescriptions

Doctors attach medicines to a patient's token directly, building a clean prescription record.

Why a static build

No sign-ups, no servers to maintain, no data leaving the device — useful for a demo, a viva, or a clinic with unreliable connectivity.

Works offline

Once loaded, every page keeps working without an internet connection.

Deploys anywhere

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — host it on GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel in minutes.

Transparent data

Everything lives in the browser's local storage, visible and resettable at any time.
